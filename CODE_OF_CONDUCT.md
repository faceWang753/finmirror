# Community Conduct

FinMirror is a technical and research community. Participate with respect, intellectual
honesty, and care for people affected by financial AI.

Expected behavior:

- critique methods and evidence, not people;
- disclose conflicts, data provenance, gold access, and result limitations;
- welcome corrections and credit contributors;
- protect private, confidential, and embargoed information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and unwanted contact;
- do not manipulate stars, reviews, citations, or benchmark results.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports should be
made privately through the repository maintainer contact once published. Enforcement
should be proportionate, documented, and protect reporter privacy.
