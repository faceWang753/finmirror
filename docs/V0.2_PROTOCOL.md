# v0.2 Calibration-Slice Protocol

## Status and claim boundary

This document defines infrastructure and release gates for a future real-source pilot.
The committed v0.2 ledger contains **candidate source records only**. No source artifact
has been admitted to a benchmark, no case has been expert-validated, and no model result
is implied.

Synthetic v0.1 remains the only scored FinMirror dataset. A candidate URL, a successful
download, or a valid content hash is not evidence that redistribution is permitted or
that a source is suitable for evaluation.

## Research question

Do paired causal and provenance metrics expose financially material failures that are
not predicted by pointwise answer accuracy or citation quality on observable,
expert-reviewed, real-source evidence?

The first calibration slice should answer whether the curation and evaluator contracts
are viable before the larger 50–100-group pilot in the research roadmap.

## Proposed calibration slice

- 12 complete paired groups across Statistics Canada and Bank of Canada source families;
- English and French review where the source and task support both official languages;
- one reference world and six atomic interventions per retained group;
- two independent finance-capable annotators and one blinded adjudicator;
- source bytes, terms snapshot, renderer, annotations, and evaluator version pinned;
- no model output visible during gold authoring or intervention validation.

This is a stop/go slice, not a representative finance benchmark. Scaling begins only
after every release gate below passes.

## Source lifecycle

1. **Nominate.** Add a `candidate` receipt with an official resource URL, terms URL,
   proposed attribution, stable resource ID, and conservative legal fields.
2. **Capture.** Retrieve an explicitly bounded artifact through a separately reviewed
   process. Preserve its exact bytes and record timestamp, byte count, and SHA-256.
   FinMirror's source module performs no network access.
3. **Review rights.** Snapshot and hash the applicable terms. Inspect the exact record
   for third-party content, logos, personal information, and provider-specific limits.
   Decide `redistribute` or `fetch_only`; `undetermined` blocks release.
4. **Render.** Produce the evaluator-visible document through a versioned extractor and
   bind its bytes, process identity, process hash, disclosure, source receipt, and
   provider-capture parent in `sources/v0.2/evidence-manifest.json`. The generic lineage
   gate exists; provider-specific extractors and rendered pilot artifacts do not.
5. **Author.** Build the reference task from the captured source. Record entity, period,
   metric, unit, minimum sufficient evidence, formula, operands, and `as_of` date.
6. **Intervene.** Create one observable atomic transformation per world. A transformed
   world must be labeled as evaluator-authored counterfactual evidence; it must never be
   presented as an authentic government publication.
7. **Annotate.** Run independent labeling and adjudication under
   `docs/ANNOTATION_GUIDE.md`. Keep model predictions hidden.
8. **Freeze.** Pin the ledger digest, dataset digest, code commit, schema version,
   annotation artifacts, and model-run plan before evaluation.

## Release gates

A source receipt may enter a scored release only when `load_ledger(...,
require_release_ready=True)` succeeds. This requires:

- exact source bytes, retrieval timestamp, byte count, and SHA-256;
- explicit `as_of` date;
- a hash-bound snapshot of the terms reviewed;
- a final `redistribute` or `fetch_only` decision;
- third-party review marked `clear` or `excluded`;
- stable source and terms URLs, licence reference, and attribution text.

The complete slice additionally requires:

- 100% schema, manifest, receipt, and deterministic replay validity;
- `finmirror evidence-status --require-real-source` succeeds, proving that a
  rights-reviewed provider capture reaches evaluator-visible derived evidence;
- observable, answerable, material, and atomic intervention approval;
- raw agreement at least 0.90 and Cohen's kappa at least 0.80 for categorical
  answerability and relation judgments;
- 100% adjudication of disagreements;
- evaluator mutation tests for every scored contract field;
- a public correction log and an explicit negative-result path.

If licensing remains unresolved, annotation agreement misses the target, or valid
financial equivalents are systematically mis-scored, stop the pilot and publish the
failure rather than expanding the dataset.

## Contamination and evaluation

Public cases are development data. A later model study must lock prompts and analysis
before producing a small refreshed test set, isolate or control web search, preserve
retrieval/tool receipts, run at least three stochastic trials, and report group-clustered
uncertainty. Use `contamination-limited`, never `contamination-free`.

## Explicit non-claims

This infrastructure does not establish:

- expert validation, native-language validity, or real-world representativeness;
- permission to redistribute any candidate artifact;
- reliability or safety of Statistics Canada, Bank of Canada, Cohere, or any model;
- endorsement by a source provider, employer, paper author, or upstream maintainer; or
- that a byte hash proves semantic correctness, provenance completeness, or legal rights.

The current evidence manifest is intentionally `synthetic_only`. Its ledger contains
candidate source families, but candidate URLs and metadata are not real-source evidence.
